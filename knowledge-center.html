Knowledge Center

Learn Soonest in minutes

Short video guides for building, managing, and publishing your schedule. Watch them in order if you're new, or jump straight to what you need.

For Staff

Preferences

Setting Your Preferences

How do I set my general availability?

Set your work days, locations, and day and time preferences so your schedule fits your life.

How do I submit a PTO request?

Request time off and see your available, accrued, and pending hours before you submit.

Shifts

Managing Your Shifts

How do I swap or release a shift?

Release a shift or request a direct swap with a coworker.

How do I pick up an open shift?

Find open shifts, claim one, or offer a trade for it.

Communication

Staying Connected

How do I use messaging?

Start conversations, create groups, and message your team inside Soonest.

How do I complete my post shift Pulse check in?

Submit your quick wellness check in after each shift and see why it matters.

How do I manage my notifications?

Choose how and when Soonest notifies you about schedules, requests, and messages.

For Admins

Step 1

Building Your Schedule

How do I create a shift?

Start here. This video shows you how to create a new shift on your schedule.

How do I manually assign shifts?

When you want to place a specific person on a specific shift yourself, this video shows you how.

How do I update a shift?

Need to change a time, assignment, or detail on an existing shift? This video walks through updating shifts.

How do I copy and paste shifts?

Save time by reusing shifts you have already built instead of recreating them from scratch.

Step 2

Running the Solver

How do I run the solver?

The solver builds your schedule automatically based on your rules and preferences. This video shows you how to run it.

Step 3

Cleaning Up the Schedule

How do I clear shifts?

Clearing removes assignments while keeping the shifts in place, so you can start fresh without rebuilding.

How do I delete shifts?

Deleting removes shifts from the schedule entirely. This video shows you how, and when to delete versus clear.

Step 4

Publishing Your Schedule

How do I publish or unpublish a schedule?

Publishing makes the schedule visible to your team. This video covers publishing, and how to unpublish if you need to make changes.

Step 5

Settings and Views

How do I work with filters?

Filters help you focus on exactly the shifts and people you want to see. This video covers how to use them.

How do I edit calendar settings?

Adjust how your calendar looks and behaves so it fits the way your team works.

How do I work with layers?

Layers let you organize and view different parts of your schedule together. This video shows you how to use them.

Approvals

Requests and Approvals

How do I review and approve requests?

Find pending PTO, swap, and release requests and approve or deny them, one at a time or in bulk.

Still need help?

If you have a question these videos don't cover, our team is happy to walk you through it.
