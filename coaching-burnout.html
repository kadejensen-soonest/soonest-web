How It Works

Pulse AI finds it. Coaching fixes it.

Most burnout programs run on guesswork. Generic resilience webinars, one off wellness weeks, surveys nobody acts on. Soonest works differently because the data does the targeting for you.

Pulse AI flags the signal

Individual risk scores, team friction patterns, declining wellbeing trends. The data tells you exactly who and what.

Coaching responds

A program matched to that exact signal. Individual recovery, team intervention, or org wide work depending on what the data shows.

Programs

Four burnout focused programs.

Each program is delivered by healthcare experienced facilitators and customized to your group. Run them standalone, or trigger them automatically when Pulse signals call for it.

Individual Burnout Recovery

One on one coaching for providers identified as high risk. Confidential, structured, and designed to move someone from disengagement back to sustainable practice.

Resilience & Recovery Cohorts

Small group programs for staff at moderate burnout risk. Peer based, evidence informed, and built around the realities of clinical work. Strong fit for ED and urgent care teams.

Psychological Safety

Build environments where clinicians speak up about errors, ask for help, and challenge decisions. The foundation that lets every other wellness investment actually stick.

Org Wide Burnout Response

For groups in a full blown retention crisis. We assess across departments, design a multi quarter intervention, and track outcomes through Pulse to verify the work is landing.

Outcomes

What this work actually delivers.

Coaching is only valuable if the data moves. Every engagement is measured against the Pulse signals that triggered it. Here's what good outcomes look like.

↓ Attrition
Retain your providers

Catching burnout early keeps your most experienced clinicians in the seat. One retained physician can offset the cost of an entire engagement.

↑ Safety
Fewer clinical errors

Teams with restored psychological safety report incidents earlier, escalate sooner, and make fewer downstream mistakes. Wellness work is patient safety work.

↑ Engagement
Measurable culture lift

Pulse scores trend up over consecutive cycles. Themes shift from frustration to ownership. Open positions get easier to fill because staff stop quietly looking.
