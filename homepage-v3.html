The Platform

Built for the complexity of medical scheduling.

Unlike generic schedulers, Soonest handles multi-location, multi-role, preference-based workflows out of the box.

Preference-based AI scheduling that builds itself

Staff set preferences, admins set rules, the solver handles the rest. Multi-location, multi-role schedules resolved simultaneously — no manual rework, no domino effect.

  • Auto-generates schedules from templates and preferences
  • Cross-location scheduling — all sites resolve at once
  • Schedule Grade — system rates fairness and constraint coverage
  • Draft → Review → Publish workflow — you stay in control
  • Staff see exactly which preferences were honored and why

Trades, releases, and call-outs — handled without you

Three distinct workflows for shift changes. Staff self-serve through the platform. You set whether admin approval is required.

  • Trade: 1-for-1 swaps, direct or org-wide
  • Release: "I'd prefer not to work this, but I will if no one picks it up"
  • Call-out: Last-minute illness with immediate admin notification
  • Open shift marketplace — post to org or specific groups
  • "Need staff?" button connects to locum tenens partner

Clock in, clock out, export to payroll — done.

Time tracking built directly into the scheduling platform. Geofencing ensures staff can only clock in from the facility. Export directly to ADP, Paycom, Paylocity, or any provider.

  • Clock in/out from mobile or desktop with geofencing
  • Automatic overtime and differential calculations
  • Export to ADP, Paycom, Paylocity, and others
  • Real-time hours tracking by department and location
  • Flag discrepancies before payroll runs

Know how your team is doing before someone quits

A 30-second end-of-shift check-in. Staff rate how heavy the shift felt, optionally share what drove it. AI surfaces burnout signals, safety flags, and at-risk staff — before it becomes a retention problem.

  • "How heavy was today?" — 1–10 scale, voice or text response
  • Anonymous posting option — staff share what they actually feel
  • AI sentiment analysis, topic clustering, smart safety alerts
  • Predictive risk scoring by department and individual
  • Connected coaching recommendations for at-risk staff
