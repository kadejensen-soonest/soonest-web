The Platform

The all-in-one scheduling platform for pediatric offices

Pediatricians and support staff set preferences, trade shifts, clock in, and check in after each shift, and Soonest uses that information to shape the schedule and recommend coaching.

Schedules that build from preferences and your office's rules

Pediatricians and support staff set preferences, admins set rules, and the solver handles the rest. Multi-role, multi-site schedules resolve at the same time.

  • Auto-generates schedules from templates and preferences
  • Cross-site scheduling, so every location resolves at once
  • Schedule Grade: the system rates fairness and constraint coverage
  • Draft, Review, Publish workflow, with you in control
  • Pediatricians and staff see exactly which preferences were honored and why

Trades, releases, and call-outs, resolved in the platform

Pediatricians and staff self-serve, and you set whether admin approval is required.

  • Trade: 1-for-1 swaps, direct or office-wide
  • Release: "I'd prefer not to work this, but I will if no one picks it up"
  • Call-out: last-minute illness with immediate admin notification
  • Open shift marketplace, posted to the office or to specific groups
  • "Need staff?" button connects to a locum tenens partner

Time tracking and payroll export, built into the schedule

Hours are tracked inside the scheduling platform and export directly to ADP, Paycom, Paylocity, or any provider.

  • Clock in and out from mobile or desktop with geofencing
  • Automatic overtime and differential calculations
  • Export to ADP, Paycom, Paylocity, and others
  • Real-time hours tracking by department and location
  • Flag discrepancies before payroll runs

Payroll & Clock

A 30-second check-in that shows who is stretched thin

After each shift, pediatricians and support staff rate how heavy it felt and can add what drove it. Pulse AI surfaces burnout signals, safety flags, and at-risk staff, so you can act before it becomes a retention problem.

  • "How heavy was today?" 1 to 10 scale, voice or text response
  • Anonymous posting option
  • AI sentiment analysis, topic clustering, smart safety alerts
  • Predictive risk scoring by department and individual
  • Connected coaching recommendations for at-risk staff

Coaching matched to what your own check-ins show

Pulse AI identifies what your team needs, and coaching delivers it, with programs recommended from your office's own data.

  • Burnout Recovery: 8-week 1:1 program for decompression and sustainable pacing
  • Resilience Training: stress management, mental health first aid, recovery skills
  • Team Dynamics: facilitation to rebuild trust, handoffs, and communication norms
  • Workplace Safety: training for aggressive patient encounters and escalation
  • Leadership: new manager foundations and executive development
