The Problem

Most schedules are built for compliance, not the nurse.

Short-staffed shifts, mandatory overtime, floating to units they've never worked, self-scheduling requests that get overridden. Each one gets justified as compliance or coverage. None of it accounts for the nurse working the shift.

Preferences disappear into the system

Preference requests go into a spreadsheet and come out unrecognizable. When a nurse can't shape their own schedule, or flag that they're stretched too thin, that gets absorbed silently, shift after shift.

Self-scheduling requests routinely overridden

Nurse managers are buried

Building a compliant, fair schedule across med-surg, ICU, and ED and other units while covering call-outs, approving swaps, and chasing down open shifts eats the week before it even starts.

15+ hours lost per scheduling cycle

The toll compounds

Twelve-hour shifts, short staffing, and back-to-back stretches don't reset between weeks. By the time it shows up as a resignation letter, it's been building for months.

The Platform

AI Scheduling

Preference-based nurse scheduling that builds itself

Nurses set preferences, admins set rules, and the solver handles the rest. Multi-unit, multi-role schedules are resolved simultaneously.

  • Auto-generates nurse schedules from templates and preferences
  • Cross-unit scheduling — all units resolve at once
  • Schedule Grade — system rates fairness and constraint coverage
  • Draft → Review → Publish workflow — you stay in control
  • Nursing staff see exactly which preferences were honored and why
Shift Management

Trades, releases, and call-outs, without the admin work

There are three distinct workflows for shift changes. Nurses self-serve through the platform, and you set whether admin approval is required.

  • Trade: 1-for-1 swaps, direct or unit-wide
  • Release: "I'd prefer not to work this, but I will if no one picks it up"
  • Call-out: last-minute illness with immediate admin notification
  • Open shift marketplace — post to a unit or facility-wide
  • "Need staff?" button connects to a staffing partner
Payroll & Clock

Clock in, clock out, export to payroll. Done.

Time tracking is built directly into Soonest. Export directly to ADP, Paycom, Paylocity, or any provider.

  • Clock in/out from mobile or desktop with geofencing
  • Automatic overtime and differential calculations
  • Export to ADP, Paycom, Paylocity, and others
  • Real-time hours tracking by unit and facility
  • Flag discrepancies before payroll runs
Pulse AI

Know how your team is doing before retention is at risk

Nurses rate how heavy the shift felt and optionally share what drove it with a 30-second end-of-shift check-in. Pulse AI surfaces burnout signals, safety flags, and at-risk staff, so you can act before it becomes a retention problem.

  • "How heavy was today?" — 1–10 scale, voice or text response
  • Anonymous posting option — staff share what they actually feel
  • AI sentiment analysis, topic clustering, smart safety alerts
  • Predictive risk scoring by unit and individual
  • Connected coaching recommendations for at-risk staff
Coaching

Targeted support driven by your team's own signals

Pulse AI identifies what your team needs, and expert coaching delivers it. From 1:1 burnout recovery to team-level facilitation and safety training, Soonest offers programs recommended based on your real data.

  • Burnout Recovery: 8-week 1:1 program for decompression and sustainable pacing
  • Resilience Training: stress management, mental health first aid, recovery skills
  • Team Dynamics: facilitation to rebuild trust, handoffs, and communication norms
  • Workplace Safety: training for aggressive patient encounters and escalation
  • Leadership: new manager foundations, charge nurse programs, executive development
