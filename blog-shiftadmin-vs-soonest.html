ShiftAdmin was the right answer ten years ago. It brought rules-based auto-generation to emergency medicine, and for a while that was enough. When QGenda acquired ShiftAdmin in 2021, innovation slowed. Today, ShiftAdmin remains an engine that requires weeks of rule configuration before it produces a usable schedule, a steep learning curve for whoever owns it, and a schedule that still needs hours of manual cleanup after every run.

Soonest is the answer for healthcare teams today. Built for both admins and providers, Soonest uses AI to build the schedule from staff preferences and your rules. A scorecard shows how the schedule performed and guides improvement. Your staff get a live AI agent for everything about their schedule, so they actually use it. Pulse captures instant feedback, and Soonest helps you forecast labor need — and it costs less.
