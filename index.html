The Problem

Burnout is destroying your team. Here's what it actually looks like.

It's not a willpower issue. It's a system design failure — and it operates on multiple levels simultaneously.

Staff aren't heard

Rotating schedules with zero input wreck providers' health, sleep, and personal lives. There's no structured outlet for what they experience on shift. When providers can't shape their schedule or flag what's happening, moral injury follows — and burnout isn't far behind.

47% of physicians report burnout

Admins are buried

15 to 20+ hours per scheduling cycle spent chasing preferences, managing rework, and handling swap requests. That's before EHR updates, compliance tasks, and everything else already on their plate. The burden is relentless and it never fully resets.

4+ full workweeks lost per year

Emotional toll compounds

High-acuity clinical work carries a cumulative emotional weight that doesn't reset between shifts. Without structured support or space to decompress, that toll compounds. Moral injury builds quietly when providers feel unheard or unsupported — and by the time it shows up in turnover, it's been years in the making.

$750K+ to replace one physician

The Platform

Everything your team needs. One platform.

From AI-generated schedules to real-time wellness intelligence and targeted coaching — built for the complexity of medical organizations.

Preference-based scheduling that builds itself

Staff set preferences, admins set rules, the solver handles the rest. Multi-location, multi-role schedules resolved simultaneously — no manual rework, no domino effect.

  • Auto-generates schedules from templates and preferences
  • Cross-location scheduling — all sites resolve at once
  • Schedule Grade — system rates fairness and constraint coverage
  • Draft → Review → Publish workflow — you stay in control
  • Staff see exactly which preferences were honored and why

Trades, releases, and call-outs — handled without you

Three distinct workflows for shift changes. Staff self-serve through the platform. You set whether admin approval is required.

  • Trade: 1-for-1 swaps, direct or org-wide
  • Release: "I'd prefer not to work this, but I will if no one picks it up"
  • Call-out: Last-minute illness with immediate admin notification
  • Open shift marketplace — post to org or specific groups
  • "Need staff?" button connects to locum tenens partner

Clock in, clock out, export to payroll — done.

Time tracking built directly into the scheduling platform. Geofencing ensures staff can only clock in from the facility. Export directly to ADP, Paycom, Paylocity, or any provider.

  • Clock in/out from mobile or desktop with geofencing
  • Automatic overtime and differential calculations
  • Export to ADP, Paycom, Paylocity, and others
  • Real-time hours tracking by department and location
  • Flag discrepancies before payroll runs

Know how your team is doing before someone quits

A 30-second end-of-shift check-in. Staff rate how heavy the shift felt and optionally share what drove it. Pulse AI surfaces burnout signals, safety flags, and at-risk staff — so you can act before it becomes a retention problem.

  • "How heavy was today?" — 1–10 scale, voice or text response
  • Anonymous posting option — staff share what they actually feel
  • AI sentiment analysis, topic clustering, smart safety alerts
  • Predictive risk scoring by department and individual
  • Connected coaching recommendations for at-risk staff

Targeted support driven by your team's own signals

Pulse AI identifies what your team needs. Coaching delivers it. From 1:1 burnout recovery to team-level facilitation and safety training — programs are recommended based on your actual data, not guesswork.

  • Burnout Recovery: 8-week 1:1 program for decompression and sustainable pacing
  • Resilience Training: Stress management, mental health first aid, recovery skills
  • Team Dynamics: Facilitation to rebuild trust, handoffs, and communication norms
  • Workplace Safety: Training for aggressive patient encounters and escalation
  • Leadership: New manager foundations, charge nurse programs, executive development

Who We Serve

Built for healthcare organizations managing clinical schedules in-house.

Not a general-purpose scheduler dressed up in a white coat. Soonest was built by and for medical providers who lived this problem.

Physician Groups

Physician-owned groups managing clinical coverage at one or more facilities. This includes emergency medicine groups, cardiology groups, anesthesiology groups, etc.

Urgent Care Chains

Regional and independent multi-location urgent care groups managing cross-location scheduling complexity, coverage gaps, and seasonal demand shifts.

Hospital Systems

Multi-department health systems managing scheduling across physicians, nursing, and support staff at scale — with regulatory compliance, float pools, and volume complexity.

Specialty Practices

Groups spanning multiple locations with complex scheduling rules, shared support staff, and varying shift structures across sites.

Freestanding Emergency Centers

Independent FECs requiring 24/7 EM-trained coverage at one or multiple locations, often with lean admin teams and physician-owned structures.

Physician Staffing Groups

Groups managing clinical coverage across multiple client facilities — with per-site rule sets and the coordination overhead that comes with contracted staffing.
