Methodology

What this is built on

Every figure in this calculator is derived from published national benchmarks, not proprietary assumptions. Here are the sources behind each input.

Turnover & Replacement Costs

Turnover rates and replacement costs across nursing, clinical, and support roles are sourced from the 2026 NSI National Health Care Retention Report, the industry's most widely cited benchmark for healthcare workforce data.

Healthcare Wage Data

Average salaries and total compensation data are drawn from the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics Occupational Employment and Wage Statistics and the BLS Employer Costs for Employee Compensation survey, which sets the benefits load multiplier used in the model.

A Note on These Estimates

Every figure here is a national benchmark estimate, not a projection for your specific organization. Actual costs vary by market, role mix, and operating model. The real number is the one we build with you on a call, using your own data as the inputs.
