Give staff a voice without losing control

Physicians log their shift preferences, hard constraints, and time-off requests directly in Soonest. The system honors them within the parameters you define — staff feel heard, admins stay in control.

A complete schedule view built for EM groups

See every shift, every physician, every coverage gap at a glance. Filter by role, location, or time period. One-click publishing with automatic staff notifications.

Coverage gaps caught before they become problems
